## Description
Relax keeps your important files safe on USB drives, external hard disks and other folders, simply and reliably, without the cloud.

Compare and preview
• Compare two folders by date and size, or byte by byte.
• See every difference in a clear side-by-side list before anything happens.
• Right-click any item to change what happens to it.

Choose how to back up
• Update (incremental): copy only new and changed files. Nothing is ever deleted.
• Mirror: make the backup an exact copy of your folder.
• Two-way: keep two folders the same, in both directions.
• Custom: decide for each kind of difference.

Safe by design
• Every file is copied to a temporary name first and renamed only when it is complete.
• Deleted and replaced files go to the Recycle Bin or a versions folder.
• Nothing is deleted when a source folder or drive is missing or empty.
• Free disk space is checked before starting.

Convenient
• Save your backups as jobs and open them with one click.
• Run jobs automatically every day, every week or when you sign in.
• Filters for files and folders you never want to back up.
• A log of every backup.

100% offline and private
Relax never connects to the internet. No account, no ads, no tracking. Your files stay on your PC and your drives.

Free to use, with no account, no ads and no tracking. Your files, jobs and backups are never touched or locked.

## Notes for certification (Partner Center > Submission options)
- Relax is a classic Win32 desktop backup and folder-synchronization program, packaged with the runFullTrust capability. It needs it to read and copy files in any folder or drive the user chooses (including USB drives) and to start scheduled backups through Windows Task Scheduler. It never asks for administrator rights.
- It uses no network access of its own.
- To test: choose two folders, press Compare, then Synchronize. Scheduled backups use Windows Task Scheduler through the RelaxBackup.exe alias.
