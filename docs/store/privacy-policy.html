Privacy Policy

Relax - Your data is safe! · Vidyasagar Academy · Last updated: 19 October 2026

Relax - Your data is safe! ("Relax") is a backup and folder synchronization program for Windows, developed by Prof. Dattaraj S. Vidyasagar and Prof. Yash D. Vidyasagar for Vidyasagar Academy.

Relax collects no personal data

Relax works 100% offline. It does not collect, store, send or share any personal information. It contains no advertising, no analytics, no tracking and no telemetry, and it never connects to the internet.

Your files stay on your devices

Relax only reads and copies the files and folders that you choose, between drives and folders on your own computer or connected storage (for example USB drives or external hard disks). Your files are never uploaded anywhere.

Information stored on your computer

To work, Relax keeps a few small files on your own computer only: your settings, the backup jobs you save, the logs of your synchronizations, and a record of the last synchronization of each folder pair. You can delete them at any time by uninstalling Relax or deleting its folder in your user profile.

Links

The About and Help menus contain links to our website and e-mail address. They open only when you click them, in your own web browser or e-mail program.

Children

Relax does not collect any information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, the new version will be published on this page with a new date.
