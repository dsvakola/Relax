Privacy Policy

Relax - Your data is safe! · Vidyasagar Academy · Last updated: 10 November 2026

Relax - Your data is safe! ("Relax") is a backup and folder synchronization program for Windows, developed by Prof. Dattaraj S. Vidyasagar and Prof. Yash D. Vidyasagar for Vidyasagar Academy.

Relax collects no personal data

Relax works 100% offline. It does not collect, store, send or share any personal information. It contains no advertising, no analytics, no tracking and no telemetry, and it contains no code that connects to the internet by itself.

Your files stay on your devices

Relax is a full-trust Windows desktop program, so it can read and write the folders and drives that you choose. Microsoft therefore lists it as an app that can access personal files. Relax uses that access only for the backup you start, on your own PC, and it collects, stores, sends and shares none of it.

Relax only reads and copies the files and folders that you choose, between drives and folders on your own computer or connected storage (for example USB drives or external hard disks). Your files are never uploaded anywhere.

Information stored on your computer

To work, Relax keeps a few small files on your own computer only: your settings, the backup jobs you save, the logs of your synchronizations, and a record of the last synchronization of each folder pair. They are kept in the folder %APPDATA%\in.edu.vsa.relax of your Windows user profile (for the Microsoft Store version Windows may keep that folder inside the app's own package folder), and you can delete them at any time. Your backups are never removed by Relax or by uninstalling it.

Microsoft Store

Relax is free. If you get it from the Microsoft Store, the download and installation are handled by Windows and the Microsoft Store under the Microsoft Privacy Statement. Relax itself sends nothing about you or your files, and Vidyasagar Academy does not receive your personal details.

Links

The About and Help menus contain links to our website and e-mail address. They open only when you click them, in your own web browser or e-mail program.

Children

Relax does not collect any information from anyone, including children.
