Privacy Policy

Relax - Your data is safe! · Vidyasagar Academy · Last updated: 10 November 2026

Relax - Your data is safe! ("Relax") is a backup and folder synchronization program for Windows, developed by Prof. Dattaraj S. Vidyasagar and Prof. Yash D. Vidyasagar for Vidyasagar Academy.

Relax collects no personal data

Relax works 100% offline. It does not collect, store, send or share any personal information. It contains no advertising, no analytics, no tracking and no telemetry, and it contains no code that connects to the internet by itself.

Your files stay on your devices

Relax only reads and copies the files and folders that you choose, between drives and folders on your own computer or connected storage (for example USB drives or external hard disks). Your files are never uploaded anywhere.

Information stored on your computer

To work, Relax keeps a few small files on your own computer only: your settings, the backup jobs you save, the logs of your synchronizations, and a record of the last synchronization of each folder pair. They are kept in the folder %APPDATA%\in.edu.vsa.relax of your Windows user profile, and you can delete that folder at any time. Your backups are never removed by Relax or by uninstalling it.

Microsoft Store purchase and trial

The Microsoft Store version of Relax is a paid app (Rs. 299, one-time) with a 7-day free trial. Whether the trial or purchase is active is checked by Windows and the Microsoft Store through the standard Store licence service, not by Relax. Relax receives only the answer (trial, purchased or not purchased) and sends nothing about you or your files. Microsoft handles your purchase and payment under the Microsoft Privacy Statement. Vidyasagar Academy does not receive your payment or personal details.

Links

The About and Help menus contain links to our website and e-mail address. They open only when you click them, in your own web browser or e-mail program.

Children

Relax does not collect any information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, the new version will be published on this page with a new date.
