# Privacy Policy: Relax - Your data is safe!

Last updated: 10 November 2026 · Developed by Prof. Dattaraj S. Vidyasagar & Prof. Yash D. Vidyasagar, for Vidyasagar Academy.

**Relax is 100% offline and collects nothing.**

## What Relax does with your data
Relax is a full-trust Windows desktop program, so it can read and write the folders and drives that you choose. Microsoft therefore lists it as an app that can access personal files. Relax uses that access only for the backup you start, on your own PC, and it collects, stores, sends and shares none of it.

- Relax reads and copies only the files and folders that you choose, between drives and folders on your own computer or connected storage (for example USB drives or external hard disks).
- Your files are never uploaded, sent or shared anywhere.
- Relax has no account, no advertising, no analytics, no tracking and no telemetry. It does not collect personal information of any kind, from anyone, including children.
- Relax contains no code that connects to the internet by itself.

## What is stored on your computer
Only on your own computer, Relax keeps a few small files: your settings, the backup jobs you save, the logs of your synchronizations, a record of the last synchronization of each folder pair. They are kept in the folder `%APPDATA%\in.edu.vsa.relax` of your Windows user profile (for the Microsoft Store version Windows may keep that folder inside the app's own package folder), and you can delete them at any time. Your backups and the files you copied are never removed by Relax or by uninstalling it.

## Microsoft Store
Relax is free. If you get it from the Microsoft Store, the download and installation are handled by Windows and the Microsoft Store under the [Microsoft Privacy Statement](https://privacy.microsoft.com/privacystatement). Relax itself sends nothing about you or your files, and Vidyasagar Academy does not receive your personal details.
