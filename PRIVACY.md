# Privacy Policy: Relax - Your data is safe!

Last updated: 10 November 2026 · Developed by Prof. Dattaraj S. Vidyasagar & Prof. Yash D. Vidyasagar, for Vidyasagar Academy.

**Relax is 100% offline and collects nothing.**

## What Relax does with your data
- Relax reads and copies only the files and folders that you choose, between drives and folders on your own computer or connected storage (for example USB drives or external hard disks).
- Your files are never uploaded, sent or shared anywhere.
- Relax has no account, no advertising, no analytics, no tracking and no telemetry. It does not collect personal information of any kind, from anyone, including children.
- Relax contains no code that connects to the internet by itself.

## What is stored on your computer
Only on your own computer, Relax keeps a few small files: your settings, the backup jobs you save, the logs of your synchronizations, a record of the last synchronization of each folder pair, and (in the Microsoft Store version) a note of whether your purchase or trial is active. You can delete them at any time by uninstalling Relax. The Microsoft Store version keeps them in its own protected app folder, which Windows removes when the app is uninstalled. Your backups and the files you copied are never removed by uninstalling.

## Microsoft Store purchase and trial
The Microsoft Store version of Relax is a paid app (Rs. 299, one-time) with a 7-day free trial. Whether the trial or purchase is active is checked by Windows and the Microsoft Store through the standard Store licence service, not by Relax. Relax receives only the answer ("trial, N days left", "purchased" or "not purchased"), and sends nothing about you or your files. Microsoft handles your purchase and payment under the [Microsoft Privacy Statement](https://privacy.microsoft.com/privacystatement). Vidyasagar Academy does not receive your payment or personal details.

## Links
The About and Help menus contain links to our website, to this page, to our software catalog on GitHub, and an e-mail address. They open only when you click them, in your own web browser or e-mail program.
